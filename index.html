THAI VISA SERVICES / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Visa questions,
answered.

Can you help if I’m not in Thailand yet?

Yes. Get in touch about your plans and where you are applying from. We can discuss e-Visa assistance and what you may need to prepare before travelling.

Can you help with my visa renewal?

Contact EZ VISA with your current visa type and the date your permission to stay expires. The next step may be an extension of stay or a new visa application, depending on your situation.

How much does visa assistance cost?

Fees depend on the service and your circumstances. Ask for a quote that explains the scope of assistance, service charges and any government fees before proceeding.

Is visa approval guaranteed?

No. EZ VISA provides application assistance. Thai authorities decide eligibility, approval and permitted length of stay.

What should I share in my first message?

Your nationality, current country, reason for staying in Thailand and approximate dates are a helpful starting point. If you are already here, include your current visa type and stay expiry date.
